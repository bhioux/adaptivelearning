## Detailed Milestone Breakdown

### **Month 1: Discovery, UX/UI & Governance**
* **Goals:** Establish brand identity, finalize user flows for all 4 primary roles (Student, Parent, Tutor, Admin), and define educational guardrails.
* **Key Deliverables:**
  * Complete interactive design prototypes (Web and Mobile).
  * Educational compliance and privacy framework documentation.
  * Product Requirements Document (PRD) sign-off.

---

### **Month 2: Core User Onboarding & Organization Structure**
* **Goals:** Build the foundation for user account management, class/class arm structuring, and tutor screening.
* **Key Deliverables:**
  * Role-based onboarding portals (Student, Parent, Tutor, Admin).
  * Automated AI screening workflow for tutor applicants.
  * Hierarchical setup for classes, subject divisions, and class arms.

---

### **Month 3: Learning Environment & Social Engagement**
* **Goals:** Launch the core delivery tools for both self-paced learning and live virtual classrooms, along with the interest-based side feed.
* **Key Deliverables:**
  * Interactive self-paced course modules (videos, quizzes, assignments).
  * Live virtual classroom setup with video, audio, and chat capabilities.
  * Customizable social news feed (sports, tech, fashion, culture).

---

### **Month 4: AI Monitoring, Adaptive Paths & Parental Sneak-Peek**
* **Goals:** Deploy the key intelligence features—adaptive difficulty adjustment, parental monitoring, and automated test supervision.
* **Key Deliverables:**
  * Adaptive learning engine adjusting content to learner performance.
  * Parental "Sneak-Peek" feature enabling non-intrusive 1–2 minute live class visits.
  * AI-assisted test proctoring and automated student activity logs.

---

### **Month 5: Financial Systems, Guardrails & System Compatibility**
* **Goals:** Enable financial transactions, payout tracking, automated feedback loops, and compliance with e-learning standards.
* **Key Deliverables:**
  * Invoicing, payment collection, and tutor work-verified compensation engine.
  * Automated parent progress reports and teacher feedback summaries.
  * Global e-learning compatibility integration (SCORM/xAPI/LTI support).

---

### **Month 6: Testing, Quality Assurance & Market Launch**
* **Goals:** Validate platform stability, perform accessibility and safety audits, publish mobile apps, and execute public release.
* **Key Deliverables:**
  * User Acceptance Testing (UAT) with focus groups (parents, students, tutors).
  * App Store and Google Play Store submission and approval.
  * Official platform go-live and onboarding of initial student cohort.
